The Empty Room

A local TabPFN model learned what a person does to a room, then listened to the minutes the room was supposed to be empty. These are the times it heard someone anyway. A local Gemma speaks for each presence, then tries to explain it away.

The Skeptic: DEBUNKED

Ambient light levels, combined with natural sunlight, fooled the presence model.

The investigation reveals that the primary factor convincing the presence model was the recorded light level (counterfactual). At the time of the apparition, the sun was indeed up, reaching an elevation of 22.9 degrees (sun_position). The sensor window data confirms high and consistent lux readings during the event, which is typical for a well-lit office environment receiving natural daylight. While the CO2 levels showed a steady decline over the period (sensor_window), this trend is consistent with normal ventilation in an occupied building, not necessarily indicating a lingering presence after departure. The model appears to have misinterpreted these strong ambient light readings as evidence of human activity, rather than recognizing them as standard environmental conditions for that time of day.
